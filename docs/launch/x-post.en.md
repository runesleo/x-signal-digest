## Post

You can scroll X for twenty minutes and still have nothing you could name the next morning. Bookmarks pile up. You do not go back.

This Grok Bot template uses your own Grok to read your own timeline. Once a day it sends 0–3 posts. Each one has the original link and why it is worth knowing. If none qualify, it says nothing today. It does not like, reply, or post. The posts stay in your Grok.

If that is your problem too, try it for a day. Reply with the post it should have dropped, or the one it missed.
